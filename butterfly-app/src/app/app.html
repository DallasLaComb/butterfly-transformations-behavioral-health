About

Jessica LaComb, LCSW

Jessica is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker with a deep commitment to helping individuals find clarity, resilience, and meaning through life's most difficult seasons.

With a warm, collaborative approach, Jessica creates a judgment-free environment where you can explore your thoughts and feelings at your own pace, whether you're managing anxiety, navigating major life transitions, or simply seeking greater self-understanding.

Butterfly Transformations Behavioral Health is a private practice dedicated to meaningful, personalized care. Every client deserves to feel truly heard.
